# 设备绑定问题处理步骤

> [!info] 导航 / Navigation
> [[文档中心]] · [[troubleshoot-options-level-pro|Options Level Pro 错误码]]

**检查当前设备、历史绑定和解绑入口。**

设备绑定用于保护授权，换电脑或重装环境后可能需要先解绑旧记录。

## 处理步骤

1. 进入我的设备页面，查看对应授权下的设备数量和最近使用时间。
2. 确认要解绑的是自己的旧设备；解绑前请先让旧设备上的 ATAS 完全退出，否则两台设备会互相抢占绑定位；解绑后，下次使用产品需要重新激活。
3. 如果设备列表为空但产品提示绑定异常，先重新登录账号中心，再重启 ATAS 后重试。
4. 仍然失败时，联系支持并提供订单号、产品名和错误提示截图，不要公开完整授权码。

---

## English

Device binding protects licenses. A new computer or rebuilt environment may require unbinding an old record first.

1. Open My Devices and review device count and last seen time for the license.
2. Confirm you are unbinding your own old device. Fully close ATAS on the old device before unbinding, or the two devices will compete for the binding slot. After unbinding, the product needs activation again next time.
3. If the device list is empty but the product reports a binding issue, sign in again, restart ATAS, and retry.
4. If it still fails, contact support with order ID, product name, and an error screenshot. Do not post the full key publicly.
