## 交易知识

系统学习期权合约、定价、策略、Greeks、波动率、Exposure、Flow 与组合读法。

- [[topics|按主题浏览全部课程]] — 用主题轨道查看课程之间的先后关系：市场与宏观、期权基础、订单流与微观结构、结构与波动率、Greeks、做市商敞口、风险与对冲、期货与期权衔接。
- 初级：建立市场基础 (/docs/trading-knowledge/beginner) — 从市场角色、价格发现、风险收益、贴现、经济周期与行为偏差开始，再进入期权合约、报价、盈亏、结算、流动性及 OI / Volume。
- 中级：连接市场机制 (/docs/trading-knowledge/intermediate) — 连接利率、能源、跨境资金流、统计分布、信息效率与博弈，再学习策略结构、无套利、Greeks、波动率和 ODV 敞口联读。
- 高级：建模与组合决策 (/docs/trading-knowledge/advanced) — 从行为反馈、绩效与尾部风险进入远期基础、风险中性建模、定价与波动率，再学习对冲、组合 Greeks、盈亏归因、高阶 Greeks、复杂策略与做市模型。

## English

This vault mirrors the TradingHub documentation centre: product guides, setup, account and license help, troubleshooting, and the trading-knowledge curriculum.

### Trading Knowledge

Study option contracts, pricing, strategies, Greeks, volatility, exposure, flow, and combined reads.

- *Browse Every Lesson By Topic* — Use topic tracks to see how lessons connect: markets and macro, options foundations, order flow and microstructure, structures and volatility, Greeks, dealer exposure, risk and hedging, and the futures linkage.
- *Beginner: Build Market Foundations* — Start with market roles, price discovery, risk and return, discounting, economic cycles, and behavioral biases before moving into option contracts, quotes, payoffs, settlement, liquidity, and OI / Volume.
- *Intermediate: Connect Market Mechanics* — Connect rates, energy, cross-border flows, statistical distributions, information efficiency, and strategic interaction before studying option structures, no-arbitrage, Greeks, volatility, and joined ODV exposure reads.
- *Advanced: Model And Manage Portfolios* — Move from behavioral feedback, performance, and tail risk into forward foundations, risk-neutral modeling, pricing, and volatility, then study hedging, portfolio Greeks, P&L attribution, higher-order Greeks, complex structures, and market-making models.

### Product Guides

Learn each product's workflow, reading order, setup, and usage boundaries.

- *Options Level* — Learn static options key levels and the recommended reading order.
- *Options Level Pro* — Learn static levels, the 0DTE heatmap, settings, and intraday workflow.
- *Options Data Viewer* — Use Data Dashboard and Greeks Profile in a repeatable order.
- *Risk Manager* — Set up activation, fixed risk, stop amount, RR targets, and simulation checks.
- *LuoZone* — Learn zone lines, dot density, complete-folder installation, and chart-validation order.
- *Squeezing Scanner* — Learn filters, result timing, reading order, and chart confirmation.
- *Rithmic Registration Tool* — Complete registration preparation, form entry, status checks, and support handoff.

### Account And License

Resolve activation, expiration, product matching, and device-binding issues.
